# **HOW TO GET AWAT WITH MURDER**
*American Series - 2014-2020*
> How to Get Away with Murder is an American legal drama thriller television series that aired on ABC from September 25, 2014, to May 14, 2020. The show follows Annalise Keating, a brilliant criminal defense attorney and law professor, who, along with her students, becomes embroiled in a complex murder plot that changes their lives forever. The series features a talented cast, including Viola Davis, who received critical acclaim for her role.
## **Here are the casts**
-**Viola Davis** as Annalise Keating
-**Billy Brown** as Nate Lahey
-**Jack Falahee** as Connor Walsh
-**Aja Naomi King** as Michaela Pratt
-**Matt McGorry** as Asher Millstone
-**Karla Souza as** Laurel Castillo
-**Liza Weil as** Bonnie Winterbottom
-**Rome Flynn as** Gabriel Maddox (later seasons)
-**Charlie Weber as** Frank Delfino
